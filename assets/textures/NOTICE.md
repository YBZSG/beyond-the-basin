The source material spans 2 m × 2 m and contains approximately 40 × 40 rounded 50 mm mosaic tiles. For legible architectural-scale pools, the engine intentionally uses a 4 m repeat, enlarging these to approximately 100 mm mosaics. The normal is OpenGL (+Y). Albedo is sampled as sRGB; normal and roughness are linear data. Engine integration neutralises the beige base pigment to preserve the game-authored ceramic palette and shares the same world-space UVs for all three maps. The original maps themselves are unmodified. No Poly Haven logo or preview render is included.

These maps are not assets from POOLS.
