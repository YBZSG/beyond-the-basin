The bundled maps retain the photographed surface wear, stains and grout.
The source 2K JPEGs were recompressed at quality 90; roughness was converted
to grayscale and reduced to 1K. Color and normals remain 2K. The shader rotates
the scan to align the square tiles, neutralizes its beige tint to aged white,
and varies the wet glaze roughness while retaining the dirty grout and wear.
